LittlePaws Parent Survey

Help us understand how children use technology and whether LittlePaws can encourage more movement, creativity and real-world play.

This survey takes about 3–5 minutes.
QUESTION 1 OF 10
How concerned are you about your child spending too much time passively watching screens?
QUESTION 2 OF 10

QUESTION 3 OF 10
How often does your child choose physical play, drawing, building or exploring instead of using a screen?
QUESTION 4 OF 10

QUESTION 5 OF 10

QUESTION 6 OF 10

QUESTION 7 OF 10
What problems or difficulties did you or your child experience while using LittlePaws?
QUESTION 8 OF 10
Compared with a normal video/game app, does LittlePaws provide a meaningful reason for your child to move, create, explore or play in the real world?
QUESTION 9 OF 10
If LittlePaws had more adventures and activities, how likely would your child be to use it again?
0 = Definitely would not    10 = Definitely would
